# ADR 0002 — Sentence is the primary scoring unit, not the document

## Context

The realistic misuse case the brief describes is not "a whole essay
generated by a model," it is a human draft that a model later polished —
a paragraph rewritten for flow, a sentence smoothed over. A single
document-level score cannot represent partial authorship: an essay that
is 80% human and 20% machine-polished collapses to one number either way,
and the evidence panel (C3) has nothing to point at.

## Decision

The sentence is the primary unit of measurement and scoring. Every
likelihood and stylometric feature is computed with the sentence as the
addressable span (Module 4). Document-level output is a derived summary:
sentence scores are smoothed and then segmented into contiguous passages
via change-point detection (Module 5), and the document probability is
assembled from those passages rather than computed directly.

Note this is not the same as scoring sentences *in isolation* — each
sentence still needs real left context for its likelihood features
(Module 4, 7A), so scoring runs per-paragraph and slices results back to
sentence spans.

## Alternatives considered

- **Document-level classification only.** Rejected — cannot represent the
  polished-paragraph case at all, which the brief calls the realistic one
  (C2), and gives the UI nothing to highlight.
- **Paragraph-level as the primary unit.** Rejected as too coarse — a
  single AI-smoothed sentence inside an otherwise human paragraph would be
  diluted into the paragraph average and likely fall under any reasonable
  threshold.
- **Token-level scoring.** Rejected — too fine-grained to present as
  evidence a human reader can argue with, and token-level noise would
  dominate the signal without heavy smoothing, at which point it is
  effectively sentence-level anyway.

## Consequences

- The manifest schema, feature cache, and evidence assembly are all keyed
  by (document_id, sentence_index), not just document_id.
- Segmentation (Module 5, `ruptures`/PELT) becomes a required pipeline
  stage, not an optional visualization step — it is how document-level
  numbers get produced at all.
- The polished/mixed corpus (Module 3A) must preserve sentence-level
  ground truth through revision, which is why it is generated
  paragraph-by-paragraph with alignment tracking rather than as
  whole-essay rewrites.
